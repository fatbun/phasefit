# Changelog

All notable changes to PhaseFit will be documented in this file.

---

## 2026-10-07

### Changed
- Dashboard: the weight, body fat and calorie deficit charts now cover the whole plan by default instead of only the most recent entries, so you can look back over your entire trajectory. Once a plan is long enough, a range switch (last 30 days / last 90 days / full plan) appears, and the weight and body fat changes follow the selected range and state what they are measured against

## 2026-09-12

### Added
- Healthy weight range: automatically calculated healthy weight range displayed as a visual range bar on Profile and Onboarding pages with distance feedback

## 2026-09-08

### Added
- Japanese (ja-JP) and Korean (ko-KR) full localization: UI, backend messages, and AI prompts with culturally adapted food recognition, nutritional standards, and meal examples
- AI cost multiplier badge on buttons that trigger AI calls (Onboarding & New Plan)
- Language switcher redesigned: dropdown on Landing page, horizontal chips on Settings page
- Pro plan purchase flow activated on the Pricing page
- Model pack details displayed in pricing (GLM5.2 Mix / DeepSeek V4 Pro Mix)
- Free plan feature list expanded with additional capabilities

### Changed
- Pricing page CTA updated to active checkout flow
- Pro feature list aligned across Pricing and Upgrade pages
