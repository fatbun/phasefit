# Changelog

## 2026-09-12

### Added
- Healthy weight range: automatically calculated healthy weight range displayed as a visual range bar on Profile and Onboarding pages with distance feedback

- Pro plan purchase flow activated on the Pricing page
- Model pack details displayed in pricing (GLM5.2 Mix / DeepSeek V4 Pro Mix)
- Free plan feature list expanded with additional capabilities

### Changed
- Pricing page CTA updated to active checkout flow
- Pro feature list aligned across Pricing and Upgrade pages
